Your task is to completely upgrade the visual quality and immersion of the Mini Golf game. Focus ONLY on graphics, environments, and visual polish — do NOT break existing gameplay.

GOAL:
Make the Mini Golf game feel like a high-quality mobile/desktop game with depth, lighting, and personality — similar to popular polished games.

----------------------------------------

🌍 ENVIRONMENT & MAP DESIGN

1. Replace flat maps with fully styled environments:
   - Grass should have texture (not solid color)
   - Add subtle height variation (small hills, slopes)
   - Edges should feel natural (no sharp cut-offs)

2. Add themed maps:
   - Classic Golf Course (clean grass, sand bunkers)
   - Neon/Arcade (glow edges, dark background, neon rails)
   - Beach Map (sand, water edges, palm decorations)
   - Space Map (dark void, glowing holes, floating platforms)

3. Add environmental props:
   - Rocks, bushes, trees
   - Sand traps
   - Water hazards with reflections
   - Barriers that match the theme

----------------------------------------

💡 LIGHTING & SHADING

1. Implement dynamic lighting:
   - Soft shadows under the ball and objects
   - Light direction (sun or glow source)
   - Slight shading on terrain

2. Add glow effects for:
   - Hole (subtle pulsing glow)
   - Power shots
   - Neon maps

3. Add ambient lighting:
   - Slight vignette or background fade
   - Depth-based shading

----------------------------------------

🏌️ BALL & PHYSICS VISUALS

1. Upgrade the golf ball:
   - Add texture (dimples)
   - Add realistic shine/reflection
   - Add shadow directly under ball

2. Add motion polish:
   - Smooth rolling animation
   - Slight motion blur on fast shots
   - Trail effect for strong hits (subtle, not distracting)

----------------------------------------

🧱 MATERIALS & SURFACES

1. Different surfaces should LOOK different:
   - Grass (soft, green texture)
   - Sand (grainy, lighter color)
   - Metal (shiny, reflective)
   - Wood (warm tone with grain)

2. Add bounce/impact feedback:
   - Tiny particle effect when hitting walls
   - Small sound-reactive visual feedback

----------------------------------------

🎮 UI + CAMERA POLISH

1. Improve camera:
   - Slight tilt for depth (not top-down flat)
   - Smooth follow when ball moves
   - Zoom slightly on shot

2. Shot indicator:
   - Clean aiming line with gradient
   - Power bar with color shift (green → red)

----------------------------------------

✨ POLISH DETAILS (IMPORTANT)

1. Add micro-animations:
   - Grass slightly moving
   - Water gently animating
   - Hole flag moving

2. Add sound-reactive visuals:
   - Ball drop → small glow pulse
   - Wall hit → quick flash

3. Optimize for BOTH:
   - Mobile (low lag, simplified effects if needed)
   - Desktop (full quality enabled)

----------------------------------------

🚫 RULES

- DO NOT break gameplay or physics
- DO NOT remove existing features

FINAL RESULT:
