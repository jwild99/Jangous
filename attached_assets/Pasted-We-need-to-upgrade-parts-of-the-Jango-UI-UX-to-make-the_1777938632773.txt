- More color, glow, and visual hierarchy
- Neon accents similar to the item shop
- Gradient backgrounds for top players
- Animated highlights for top ranks (#1, #2, #3)

Top 3 styling:
- #1 → Gold glow, subtle pulsing animation, crown icon
- #2 → Silver glow
- #3 → Bronze glow

- Smooth hover effects on rows

Make it feel competitive, premium, and addictive—not flat.
